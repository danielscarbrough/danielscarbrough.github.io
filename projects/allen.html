Allen Engineering Internship

During the summer of 2024, I participated in a three-month internship as a manufacturing engineer at the local company Allen Engineering Corporation in Paragould, Arkansas. Allen Engineering designs and produces concrete equipment such as trowels or bridge pavers that are sold across the world. I worked alongside four other engineers to assist in quality control and manufacturing efficiency for the company. I particularly utilized my mechanical CAD skills, using them for 3D design and FEA analysis.

A major portion of my time at Allen Engineering was spent designing and drafting fixtures and jigs for the company using Autodesk Inventor. My largest task was creating a weld fixture to fit the chassis of the company's latest and largest riding trowel. This fixture would streamline the welding process for the welders in the production facility. I consistently communicated with the welders who would be using the fixture, ensuring that the model would fit their specifications and needs, while also being simple to manufacture. The drafts for the two part fixture are pictured below, along with two smaller projects for developing a painting rack for powder coating and a shelving unit for storing specific assemblies. All shown diagrams have been approved for view by Allen Engineering Corp. These are not my only projects I completed during my internship, but I believe they are a representative sample my work.

While my internship did not last long enough for me to manage all projects from design to assembly, I oversaw the manufacturing for multiple of them. I worked with the manufacturing and engineering teams to ensure the designs matched the company's capabilities and standards. I additionally worked with the company's inventory management system in order to properly secure and track the materials necessary for production.
